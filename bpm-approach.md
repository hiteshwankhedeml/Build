# 🟢 BPM Approach

* It us to identify, analyze, measure and improve an organizations processes
* BPM is not to be seen as just another software, but rather as a strive for operational excellence
* <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**BPM Lifecycle:**</mark>
  * <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**Design: Identify and author the process on paper**</mark>
  * <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**Model: Model the process on BPM system, like approvals based on invoice amount**</mark>
  * <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**Implement**</mark>
  * <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**Monitor**</mark>
  * <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**Optimize: As per feedback improve**</mark>
  * <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**Re-engineering: Add more automation**</mark>
